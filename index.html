Pocket Plunder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

Pocket Plunder (Android package com.pocketplunder.game) is provided by Ninong Heaven. For privacy questions, contact juamandudz1590@gmail.com.

Information used on your device

Pocket Plunder is a single-player game that can be played offline. It stores your chosen captain name, flag, settings, game progress, ships, inventory and voyage information in the app's local storage so you can resume playing. A backup copy of the save may also be kept locally to recover from a damaged save.

If you use the optional demonstration shop, its simulated wallet and transaction history are stored separately on your device. The demonstration shop does not charge real money or grant purchases to your main game.

Collection and sharing

Your local captain name and game saves are not uploaded to us. This release does not provide cloud saves, advertisements or access to your contacts, precise location, camera or microphone.

Google Play Games and Google Play services

On Android, the game integrates Google Play Games Services for platform sign-in. Google may sign you in automatically according to your Play Games settings. You can also request sign-in in the game's Settings. Playing the game does not require signing in.

The Google Play Games SDK processes your gamer identity, including your player identifier, gamer name and profile avatar, and collects service analytics and diagnostics to operate and improve its services. These services use encrypted connections. Google shares your gamer identity with the game when you sign in; we do not maintain a server database of these profiles. This release does not request your friends list or upload your local captain save, achievements or leaderboard scores.

The Android build also includes Google Play Billing, which connects to Google Play and can query product availability. Real-money purchases and the separate online shop account are disabled in this release. The local demonstration shop does not process payments.

Google handles its services under the Google Privacy Policy. Profile visibility and automatic sign-in are controlled through your Google Play Games settings.

Retention, security and deletion

Game data is kept in the app's local storage while you use the game. Android's app storage protections apply; the game's save-integrity check detects damaged files but is not encryption. You can delete the locally stored game and demonstration-shop data by clearing Pocket Plunder's app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app. This removes your local progress. We do not hold a server copy from which to restore it.

To delete Google Play Games profile or game data held by Google, use your Play Games profile settings or Google Account. See Google's instructions for deleting Play Games data. Google controls retention of its service data under its privacy policy. Deleting Play Games data does not delete the local Pocket Plunder save; clear the app's storage separately to remove that save.

Support correspondence

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond to your request. Our email provider processes the correspondence to deliver and store it. We keep it only as long as needed to address your request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact juamandudz1590@gmail.com to ask about or request deletion of support correspondence.

Children's information

Pocket Plunder is intended for players aged 13 and older. Google Play Games processes data as described above when its services are used. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through a support message, contact juamandudz1590@gmail.com so we can review and address the request.

Changes

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. This policy describes the current Early Access release.
